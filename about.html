Meet Hatsune Miku!

The virtual singer who helped creators around the world turn their ideas into music.

01 / Character Profile

Basic Information

  • Name: Hatsune Miku
  • Japanese: 初音ミク
  • Birthday: August 31
  • Age: 16 (official character setting)
  • Height: 158 cm
  • Weight: 42 kg
  • Signature color: Turquoise

Character Overview

Hatsune Miku is a virtual singer whose recognizable design includes long turquoise twin-tails and a futuristic stage outfit.

Her character and voicebank are used by creators to produce songs across many musical genres.

Her popularity has also inspired illustrations, concerts, games, and countless fan creations.

02 / Her Story

Miku's story is closely connected to the development of singing-synthesis software and the online creator community.

AUG 31, 2007

The First Release

Crypton Future Media released Hatsune Miku as a singing-synthesis voicebank for the VOCALOID2 software.

CREATOR CULTURE

A Community Grows

Musicians, illustrators, animators, and fans helped Miku grow beyond software into a collaborative creative phenomenon.

LIVE PERFORMANCE

From Screen to Stage

Miku became known for live performances featuring projected or screen-based character visuals alongside musicians.

TODAY

An Icon of Digital Music

Her character continues to inspire music, games, artwork, events, and fan-made projects around the world.

03 / Did You Know?

01 — Her Name

Her name can be interpreted as "the first sound of the future."

02 — A Voice for Creators

Producers can create very different songs using the same voicebank, from cheerful pop to experimental music.

03 — Not One Singer

Her songs are made by many different creators. There is no single artist responsible for all Miku music.

04 — A Digital Collaborator

Fans contribute songs, fan art, videos, choreography, and other creative interpretations of Miku.
